How to Prepare for Flooding in Youngstown, OH

Home / Blog / Flood Damage Preparation Tips in Youngstown, OH

Nobody controls the weather, yet Youngstown, OH property owners can still get ahead of flood damage with a few proactive steps. A prepared home takes far less of a hit when it floods, and the savings in restoration costs can reach the thousands. Here Youngstown Restoration shares the preparation steps that work best for homeowners in Youngstown.

Understand the Flood Risk You Face

Preparation begins with knowing how likely your property is to flood. Look up your address on the FEMA flood maps to see if it falls inside a designated flood zone. Keep in mind that the majority of flood claims come from areas not designated as flood zones, so a lower-risk location still deserves attention. Consider drainage patterns, the lay of the land, and the distance to creeks or other waterways that heavy rain could push over their banks.

Ways to Protect the Property

The main physical steps are to seal cracks in the foundation and apply a waterproof coating, grade the soil so it slopes away from the house, install a battery-backed sump pump and keep it maintained, clear the gutters and downspouts and carry their discharge 6 feet or more away from the foundation, raise HVAC equipment and electrical panels above likely flood levels, and store valuables and important documents above ground level.

Getting Your Insurance in Order

Standard homeowner's insurance does not cover flood damage from rising water. Consider purchasing flood insurance through the NFIP or a private carrier, and since there is typically a 30-day waiting period before coverage begins, don't wait for a forecast to act. Photograph and film your home and its contents so you have a record for the insurer. Keep insurance documents accessible and know your coverage limits and deductible.

Emergency Response Plan

Have a plan in place before flooding threatens. Pack a go-bag and learn the routes you would take to evacuate. Store the Youngstown Restoration number, (979) 290-9464, in your contacts to get professional cleanup started as soon as the flood is over. Have your insurance agent's contact information readily available. When a flood is in the forecast, carry valuables upstairs, shut off utilities if you are told to, and record the condition of your property before it arrives.
