Finding Your Water Shutoff Valve in Youngstown, OH

Home / Blog / Emergency Water Shutoff Guide in Youngstown, OH

Stopping the flow of water is the quickest way to limit damage during a water emergency. Finding and working the water shutoff valve is a skill that could keep thousands of dollars in your pocket and spare your property a lot of damage. Before an emergency ever happens, each homeowner in Youngstown, OH should learn where the valve is and how to turn it.

Finding the Main Shutoff Valve in Your Home

The main shutoff in a typical Youngstown home sits in one of five places: a laundry area or utility room, a crawl space or basement by the foundation wall, an interior wall of the garage, the front of the home at the point the line comes in from the street, or a ground-level box by the street that holds the meter shutoff. Two types are typical: gate valves, which have a round handle, and ball valves, which have a lever. Ball valves are more reliable and can be turned off with a quarter turn.

How to Shut Off Water Quickly

A gate valve closes when you turn its handle clockwise, to the right, for several full turns, to the point where it will not go further. For ball valves, turn the lever handle perpendicular to the pipe. If the valve is stuck, do not force it, because you may break it and make the situation worse. In that case, shut off water at the meter using a meter key or adjustable wrench.

Fixture and Appliance Shutoffs

You can usually stop water to one fixture or appliance without cutting off the whole house, because most have a shutoff valve of their own. Check behind toilets and washing machines, beneath each sink, and next to the water heater. Learn where each one is so you can quickly stop a leak in one place and still have water everywhere else in the house.
