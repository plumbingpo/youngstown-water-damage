Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Sinks, dishwashers, washing machines and water heaters are all good spots for a leak sensor.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Carpet, drywall or hardwood has been soaked for more than a few hours.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.
